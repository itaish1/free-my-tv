Antonio-Light.ttf
  Antonio by Vernon Adams and the Antonio Project Authors (https://github.com/googlefonts/antonioFont),
  SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-Antonio.txt; no Reserved Font Name).
  Taken from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/antonio (Antonio[wght].ttf, sha256
  9e95a2258ecdf3e45c72c5bbea1c4cd350e8f7bebc87c9dba53b29b1890b8903) and fixed at weight 300 with overlapping
  contours removed (fontTools varLib.instancer), so the glass clock's outline draws clean on the TV.
  Used for the big clock, as in couch-tv (https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv, MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Seagey),
  whose look the couch-look style follows. No couch-tv code, images or files are copied; its look is redrawn
  in our own CSS (web/home.css, "couch-look").
