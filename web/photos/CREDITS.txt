Background photos (web/photos/)
===============================

Bundled in the app; nothing is downloaded at runtime. Each photo was taken from Wikimedia Commons, where it is published
under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), originally from Unsplash (photos uploaded to Unsplash before June 2017 were
released under CC0). Cropped to 16:9, resized to 1920x1080 and re-encoded as JPEG. CC0 requires no attribution; the
photographers are credited here anyway.
