Background photos (web/photos/)
===============================

Bundled in the app; nothing is downloaded at runtime. All ten come from couch-tv
(https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv, tv-home/web/backgrounds/, commit 0853604; MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Seagey),
which had already cropped and resized them to 1920x1080. The day set (brighter) shows by day, the night set (darker) at
night; home.js (PHOTOS) lists them. Changes made here: grose-valley, tessellated-pavement and vang-vieng-sunset were
re-encoded as JPEG at quality 76 to keep the app small; the other seven are couch-tv's files unchanged.

The five Wikimedia Commons photos are under Creative Commons licences that allow redistribution with attribution: the
home screen and the screensaver show the photographer and licence in a corner while each one is on screen, and this file
credits them in full. The BY-SA ones, as adapted (cropped, resized, re-encoded), stay under their same licence.
Licences checked on the Commons pages on 2 Nov 2026.
